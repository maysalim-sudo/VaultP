Privacy

Applies to the VaultP apps for Android and Windows, and to this website. Last updated 27 September 2026.

In short: VaultP collects nothing. There is no account, no server run by VaultP, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting. What you put in the app stays on your devices, encrypted, and goes nowhere you did not send it.

What the app stores, and where

Your notes, expenses, debts, documents and settings are kept in an encrypted database on the device you are using. Images and scanned documents are encrypted as separate files beside it. The key that opens them is protected by your passphrase, which is never stored and never leaves the device you typed it on.

Nobody — including the people who make VaultP — can read your vault or recover it for you. If the passphrase is lost, the vault cannot be opened.

What leaves your device

Only what you choose to send, and only encrypted:

If you…Then
Sync through a folderEncrypted segments and files are written to the folder you picked. Whatever keeps that folder in step (Syncthing, a shared drive) carries them. They cannot be read without your passphrase.
Sync through Google DriveThe same encrypted data is stored in a folder in your Google Drive, through a Google project you set up. The app can only see files it created. Google can see the files' names and sizes, not their contents. Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google account.
Export a backupA full, unencrypted copy of your vault is saved wherever you choose. Its protection is up to where you keep it; the app warns you at the button.
Save a copy of a documentA decrypted copy of that one file is written where you choose.
Do none of theseNothing leaves the device. The app works entirely offline.

Permissions the Android app asks for

PermissionUsed for
CameraTaking a photo for a note or a document, only when you tap to do so.
PhotosPicking an image from your gallery, only when you tap to do so.
NotificationsReminders you set on notes, documents and recurring charges.
FingerprintUnlocking the vault. Your fingerprint is handled by Android and never seen by the app.

The clipboard is read only when you choose to paste into a note. Text in scanned documents is read on the device itself; nothing is sent to a recognition service.

This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no scripts, and loads nothing from any other domain — no fonts, no analytics, no embeds. It is a set of static files. The hosting provider (Vercel) handles the connection and may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security and operations, under its own privacy policy.

Children

VaultP is a general-purpose tool not directed at children, and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here and in the project's repository, where every change is visible in its history.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised on the project's GitHub repository. For anything that could expose someone's vault, use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting instead — see SECURITY.md.
