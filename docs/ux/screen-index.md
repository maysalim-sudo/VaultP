## Notes

- Search is an overlay/action, not a primary destination.
- Payment methods are single-choice per expense; the default is optional and configurable.
- Accounts keep the manually checked balance as the baseline and show derived estimates separately.
- Archive and removal flows preserve history.
- The light and dark variants are separate artboards; they are not bound to FigCraft variable modes yet.
